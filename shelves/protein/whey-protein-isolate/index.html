How this shelf was built

Nine slots go to the nine highest scores. Three are reserved, because twelve products chosen purely on score would be twelve expensive, certified, flavoured tubs, and that serves one kind of buyer.

  • 10Access

    The highest-scoring product in the cheapest third of its type. A shelf that only a well-paid buyer can use is not a curated shelf, it is a boutique.

  • 11Regimen

    The highest-scoring product that satisfies the type's dietary constraint — vegan, dairy-free or vegetarian. Convictions are part of the brief.

  • 12Minimalist

    The highest-scoring unflavoured, unsweetened or shortest-ingredient product. Someone always wants the version with nothing added, and it is rarely the one that scores best overall.
