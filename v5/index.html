Four rules. No exceptions.

  1. Curated by default.

    No endless aisles, no artificial depth. If it is here, it is already validated.

  2. Science over labels.

    Organic is not a criterion. Vegan, keto, natural are not guarantees. Ingredients and doses are.

  3. No hype tolerance.

    No fashionable superfood without evidence, no miracle claims, no TikTok trends. Fashion passes. Physiology stays.

  4. Family-safe by design.

    A parent should be able to buy without doubting, justifying or explaining. If it is not acceptable for a child, it is not acceptable.

Manifesto
