The standard, the evidence, and the limits +

Published references have a readable, complete ingredient declaration checked against the applicable ingredient restrictions in charter 0.15.0. This first filter does not certify nutritional quality, efficacy or laboratory testing.

A single ingredient excluded by our applicable rules rules out the whole product, regardless of quantity or packaging claims. An unresolved ingredient or an incomplete ingredient list puts admission on hold rather than granting it: unknown is not the same as clean. These are precautionary selection decisions, not proof that an excluded product is harmful or that a published one is risk-free.

We publish all ingredient-admitted references with an available Kuwait offer, subject to recorded editorial withdrawals. The catalogue has no fixed size or brand quota. An empty type can reflect unresolved ingredients, no available Kuwait offer.

We record tests for content, metals, microbiology and banned substances separately. An anti-doping certification does not establish results in every other area, and historical reports do not establish the composition of the batch you receive. Missing evidence does not establish danger.

A Kuwait stock confirmation expires after 24 hours. We never turn a missing or failed check into a stock claim. A price is an observation, not a quote including delivery.

Product photography is the retailer’s own, shown here under test while this site is not indexed. Comparisons follow your selected preferences. This selection is for adults and does not determine individual medical suitability.

Affiliate tracking is not active. If introduced later, it will be disclosed and will not change our ingredient decisions.
