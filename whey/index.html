What this is, and what it is not.

  • We hold no stock and sell nothing. Every link goes to a merchant. A commission on some of them never touches the ranking, which is complete before any price is attached.

  • Product records are authored from published formulations and manufacturer panels, and are marked as reported where they were not re-verified against a live listing.
  • 19 products is a reading list, not a survey of the market. It will grow, and this page will change when it does.
