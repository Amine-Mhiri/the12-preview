We do not round the rejection rate up

A high rejection rate flatters us, which is exactly why it has to be computed rather than chosen. The number here is whatever the pipeline produced on its last run.

We do not count a product twice

The same SKU listed by four merchants is one product. Inflating the analysed figure with duplicate listings would make the denominator meaningless.

We do not quietly drop the near-misses

Products that cleared the filter and still did not make a shelf are published on every type page, with the reason. A shelf you cannot see the edge of is not curated.
