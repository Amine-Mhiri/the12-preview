BAccepted

A very short label with no flavouring at all. What it lacks is an independent test, not a formulation.

What we like

  • Protein at or above 80% by dry weight
  • Zero added sugar and zero non-nutritive sweetener
  • Full quantitative disclosure of every ingredient

What we would change

  • No cross-contact statement and the facility is not declared allergen-controlled
  • Brand has no identifiable legal entity, address or contactable quality function
  • Nobody independent has tested this. An unverified product can be excellent, but it cannot be a benchmark — nobody gets top marks on trust me.
