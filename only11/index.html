The standard, the evidence, and the limits +

The shortlist follows Only11 ingredient charter 0.8.1, reviewed on 29 September 2026. All 49 selected references pass the first, declarative ingredient screen. This is not a laboratory certification or a claim that every current batch has been tested.

A single ingredient excluded by our applicable charter rules rules out the whole product, regardless of quantity or packaging claims. An unresolved ingredient or incomplete ingredient list puts admission on hold. These are precautionary selection decisions, not proof that an excluded product is harmful or that a selected one is risk-free. In this review, 100 references were excluded and one remains on hold; these counts refer to products and pack sizes, not unique ingredients.

Artificial colors, artificial flavors and artificial sweeteners are excluded. Explicitly natural flavors and stevia are accepted based on their ingredient declarations. “No artificial sweeteners” does not mean unsweetened or sugar-free.

We record tests for protein content, metals, microbiology and banned substances separately. An anti-doping certification does not establish results in every other area. Historical reports do not establish the composition of the batch you receive. Links to ConsumerLab, Consumer Reports or Que Choisir are shown when our review identifies a product match, with its scope and access limits. The current review found two product/flavor matches in ConsumerLab’s public list, a historical Nutricost Grass-Fed Unflavored match in its 2022 announcement, and one historical BioChem Vanilla mention in Consumer Reports. No exact match was identified in the Consumer Reports 2025 results or the Que Choisir comparison reviewed. Missing evidence does not establish danger.

A current Kuwait stock confirmation expires after 24 hours. We never turn a missing or failed check into a stock claim. A price is an observation, not a quote including delivery.

Ranking follows your selected preferences, with no overall health score. This shortlist is intended for adults and does not determine individual medical suitability. All whey products contain milk-derived protein.

Affiliate tracking is not active. If introduced later, it will be disclosed and will not change our ingredient decisions.
